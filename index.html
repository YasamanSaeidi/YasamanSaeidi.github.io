PhD Candidate in Economics · London School of Economics

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the London School of Economics, supervised by Stephen Machin and Tom Kirchmaier. My research is in labour economics and the economics of crime.

I study how the built environment and wider economic conditions shape criminal behaviour and victimisation. My job market paper asks what happens to crime when a city builds new transport infrastructure, using recorded crime data from Greater Manchester Police alongside the Crime Survey of England and Wales. Related work examines the effect of the cost of living crisis on offending, and the role of alcohol and football in domestic abuse.

Transport Innovation and Crime

This study assesses how newly built transport infrastructure influences the temporal and spatial dynamics of crime, exploring underlying mechanisms. The research also examines variations in effects across different crime types, offenders' characteristics, and victimisation experience. Initially, the focus is on the Manchester Metrolink, a comprehensive tram system in Greater Manchester, England, using data from the Greater Manchester Police on recorded crimes. The study then extends to all areas in England and Wales with newly established tram networks, incorporating the Crime Survey of England and Wales to assess citizens' crime reporting and victimisation. This analysis offers critical insights into the relationship between transport innovation and crime, contributing to urban planning and policy decisions.

Step Together Pilot Report

Tom Kirchmaier, assisted by Shubhangi Agrawal, Julia Friedberg, Yasaman Saeidi and Ricky Wang

Youth Endowment Fund, January 2025

An evaluation of the Step Together pilot, which placed trained adult chaperones on routes used by children walking to and from school across the West Midlands. The study assesses whether a robust quantitative evaluation of the programme is feasible, using police administrative data and the National Pupil Database to examine crime, antisocial behaviour, school attendance and attainment.
